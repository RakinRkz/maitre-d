# Saffron Table menu

Saffron Table cooks modern London food with saffron, cardamom and other warm spices. The menu has
starters, mains, sides and desserts. Prices are in pounds sterling (GBP).

How to read the tables:

- **Diet** is `vegan`, `vegetarian`, or blank. A blank Diet means the dish contains meat or fish.
- **Contains** lists the allergens, from the UK's 14 allergens, that the dish contains. `none`
  means the dish contains none of them.

## Allergens and dietary requirements

- The kitchen handles all 14 of the UK's allergens. Cross-contact traces cannot be ruled out, so a
  dish that does not list an allergen may still contain traces of it.
- The feta and paneer are made with vegetarian rennet, so the dishes tagged vegetarian suit
  vegetarians.
- A guest with a severe allergy must tell the staff when booking, and again on arrival.
- The agent states only what this page says. It never guesses about a dish that is not listed here.
